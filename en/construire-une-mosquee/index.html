In Tunisia: an authorisation is required

In Tunisia, a mosque or prayer room cannot be built freely: an authorisation is needed. A written request is made to the delegation, with a certificate of donation or allocation of the land, a location plan, an architect's plan and a cost estimate. It then goes to the governorate and to the Ministry of Religious Affairs, which grants or refuses it (about 45 days in all). Law no. 88-34 of 3 May 1988 on mosques.

A public collection of donations must also be authorised: before giving to a building project near you, ask to see the authorisation.
